# Code of conduct

UseHourly follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(adapted from the Covenant, which is licensed CC BY 4.0).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion
or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Criticise the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask a question before assuming a mistake was careless.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an email address, without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban someone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report a problem privately to **admin@zontakllc.com**. All reports are handled in confidence, and we will not
retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
