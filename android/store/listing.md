## Full description
UseHourly is free time tracking for freelancers. Log a day's work in seconds, see your week at a glance, and turn your hours into an invoice.

QUICK TO USE
• Pick a project, type the time (1.5, 1:30 or 90m) and tap Add. That's it.
• Prefer a stopwatch? Start a timer and stop it when you're done.
• Day, week and month views show where your hours went.

TIMESHEETS CLIENTS CAN TRUST
• Weekly timesheet with totals per day and per project.
• Share a timesheet with a client for sign-off. They approve with one tap and you get an email.
• Export to Excel for any week, month or date range, or export everything.

INVOICES IN A MINUTE
• Make an invoice from the hours you logged, with your logo, your details and your client's.
• Download a PDF, or email it to your client with a private link.
• Mark invoices paid, and keep the paid date for tax time.
• Round billed time (for example up to the next 15 minutes) per client.
• Send invoices to Xero or QuickBooks with a CSV file.

BUILT FOR REAL FREELANCE LIFE
• Clients and projects with their own hourly rates and budgets.
• Mark holidays and unpaid days as non-billable.
• A year summary with hours, earnings, quarters and clients, handy for estimated tax payments.
• Import your history from Toggl, Clockify or any CSV.
• Works offline: add time without a connection and it syncs when you're back.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
• No ads, no paywall, no tracking of what you type. Donations are optional.
• The code is open source (MIT): github.com/Zontak-LLC/usehourly
• Delete your account and data any time from the Account page.

Made by Zontak LLC.
