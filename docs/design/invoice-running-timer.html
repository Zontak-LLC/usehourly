New invoice while a timer runs

Create invoice is never grey because of a timer. Pressed while a timer runs on this invoice's time, it asks one plain question. With no running timer on this invoice it makes the invoice at once, as today. The preview shows only stopped time; its notice now reads "⚠ A timer is running. Its time is not on this invoice until you stop it." With no time and no timer the empty preview adds "Log time on Today, or pick another period."
