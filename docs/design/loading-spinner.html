Loading: one calm spinner, only on slow waits

When a save is still open after 300 ms, the button you pressed shows this small spinner; a faster answer shows nothing, so nothing flickers. The button is off at once and says it is busy to a screen reader (aria-busy); the spinner inside it has no name of its own, so the button keeps its name ("Log time", "Save"). On Log time the spinner sits in the round disc instead of the clock icon; the words stay "Log time" (or "Logged 1:30" for its moment). The Account Save button shows the spinner and "Saving…", then "Save" again. Create invoice keeps its icon until the spinner takes its place. With reduced motion the spinner stands still.

Where it does not go: lists and cards whose shape is known keep their soft placeholder bars (skeletons), so the page does not jump when the data arrives.
