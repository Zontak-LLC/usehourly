Log time field: say what will be logged

The line under the chips is the one place the field talks. Empty: the first-use hint (unchanged). A time it can read: "= 2:00 · Day total 0:00 → 2:00 h", updated on every key. A time it cannot log: plain words in the invalid style, worked out again on every key, so the next key that makes a time clears it. Log time stays off while there is an error; Enter shows the same words in a toast. A bare number up to 24 is hours; minutes need "m".
